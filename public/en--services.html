Transport services for temperature-sensitive goods.

Choose the transport service that fits your goods, route and agreed temperature. OKT Logistics assesses each request with the information needed to plan collection and delivery.

Contact operations

Refrigerated transport

For chilled products, share the required transport conditions and packaging details. We consider the product specification alongside the loading and unloading arrangements.

Frozen transport

For frozen goods, provide the required temperature and any product-specific conditions. The agreed setting must follow the shipment specification; one example temperature is not suitable for every product.

International refrigerated transport

We assess movements between the Netherlands, Germany, Belgium and France. Precise addresses, delivery slots and access requirements help us plan a practical route.

Groupage and full truckloads

Tell us the pallet count, dimensions and gross weight. For part loads, compatibility with other goods and delivery windows is assessed before acceptance.
