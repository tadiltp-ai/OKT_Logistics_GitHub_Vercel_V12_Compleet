OKT Logistics BV

Refrigerated and frozen transport across borders.

OKT Logistics supports shippers of chilled and frozen goods with clear transport arrangements and direct contact with operations. Based in Escharen in the Netherlands, we assess domestic and cross-border requests for full truckloads and part loads.

Contact operations

Transport matched to your product

The product specification determines the agreed transport temperature. Tell us what you are shipping, how it is packed and which handling requirements apply. We discuss the route and delivery windows before confirming an assignment.

Full loads and groupage

A full truckload can suit a complete trailer or a shipment requiring a dedicated journey. For groupage, we assess compatibility between the route, product conditions, available capacity and delivery schedule.

The Netherlands, Germany, Belgium and France

Our focus is refrigerated and frozen road transport in these four countries. Send the exact collection and delivery addresses rather than only a region, so operations can assess your request.

Food logistics with direct communication

For chilled foods and frozen products, preparation at collection and coordination with the receiver matter. Agree the loading slot, unloading slot and site contacts before departure.
