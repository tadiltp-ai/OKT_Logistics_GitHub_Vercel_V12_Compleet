OKT Logistics BV

Refrigerated transport between the Netherlands and Germany.

OKT Logistics assesses chilled and frozen shipments between the Netherlands and Germany. Send your collection and delivery details to operations for a route-specific assessment of price and availability.

Contact operations

Plan your shipment to Germany

Specify the exact receiving address in Germany, the requested delivery date and the available unloading window. For return movements, provide the same information for the collection site and destination in the Netherlands.

Chilled and frozen goods

State the required temperature and product specification. Include the packaging, pallet dimensions, total weight and any restrictions on combining the goods with other shipments.

Loading and receiving arrangements

For deliveries to German warehouses and distribution centres, confirm the site contact, dock booking and receiving hours. Include any access requirements in your request so the journey can be assessed with the delivery slot in mind.

A quote based on your actual route

We assess full truckloads and groupage according to available capacity, timings and compatible product conditions. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking; operations will discuss the proposed transport with you.
