Refrigerated transport built around your shipment.

Chilled goods require a clear agreement on product conditions and timing. OKT Logistics assesses refrigerated transport requests for domestic and international routes, including full truckloads and compatible part loads.

Contact operations

Product conditions come first

Specify the product, packaging and required transport temperature. The vehicle setting and the temperature of the goods at loading are different considerations. Agree the preparation of the goods with the collection site.

Collection and delivery coordination

Provide a loading slot, unloading slot and contact person at each location. Include booking references and any access restrictions so the receiving team can prepare.

Full load or part load

For a full trailer, send the pallet layout and total weight. For groupage, we also assess whether the route, timings and temperature conditions fit a combined movement.

Request a practical assessment

Include collection and delivery addresses, dates, pallet dimensions and product details. Operations will assess feasibility and availability before confirming a transport assignment.
