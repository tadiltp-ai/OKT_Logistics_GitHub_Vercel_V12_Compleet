OKT Logistics BV

Frozen transport with clear product and delivery requirements.

OKT Logistics assesses frozen goods transport in the Netherlands and towards Germany, Belgium and France. Share the product specification and schedule so operations can agree the conditions for your shipment.

Contact operations

Agree the required temperature

There is no single setting for every frozen product. Use the product specification and transport order to establish the required condition. Tell us about any additional handling restrictions before booking.

Prepare the collection

Have the goods ready for the agreed slot, with suitable packaging and accessible shipment details. Share the dock reference and collection contact so loading can be coordinated.

Plan the handover

Provide the receiver’s delivery window, address and booking reference. Clear unloading arrangements help the driver and warehouse coordinate the handover.

Quantities and capacity

We assess full loads and part loads using pallet count, dimensions, weight and route. Availability and compatibility are confirmed for each individual request.
