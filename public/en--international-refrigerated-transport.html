International refrigerated transport with clear coordination.

Cross-border refrigerated transport combines product conditions with route planning and receiving arrangements. OKT Logistics is based in Escharen and focuses on the Netherlands, Germany, Belgium and France.

Contact operations

One complete transport request

Send both full addresses, collection and delivery dates, product details, temperature, pallet quantities and gross weight. Add the contact details of the loading and receiving sites.

Site requirements across borders

Include access instructions, booking numbers and time windows. A location may have its own reception process, so confirm these arrangements with the shipper and receiver before departure.

Transport documents

Make sure shipment references and the required accompanying documents match the goods and delivery address. Discuss product-specific requirements before agreeing the journey.

Full truckload and groupage

The appropriate transport format depends on quantity, route, capacity and compatible product conditions. We assess each request before confirming availability and price.
